Egg Tower Night · Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Updated October 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Egg Tower Night can be played offline and does not require registration or login. This version does not actively collect your name, phone number, location, advertising identifier or device identifier. We do not upload saves, coins, collections, inputs or game statistics to a developer server. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. How Information Is Used

Progress and settings are used only to restore gameplay and preferences on your device. They are not used for advertising profiles, cross-app tracking or sale to third parties. Information you voluntarily send us is used to answer your inquiry and investigate issues, and retained for the time needed to handle the inquiry. You may request deletion at the email below, except where retention is legally required.

3. Local Data Storage

Progress, coins, animal collections, training and settings are stored in the app sandbox. You can delete your game save in Settings. Uninstalling may erase your progress. iOS or Android may back up app data according to your system settings; the operating system manages that process. We do not provide account-based cloud saves and cannot remotely restore deleted saves.

4. Third-Party SDKs and System Services

This version uses the Unity game engine and UI components. No advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party login, online payment or social SDK is integrated. Unity advertising, analytics and crash-upload services are disabled. The privacy page uses a system WebView to read a bundled local HTML file without remote scripts, images or tracking code. Email and App Store rating features open only as you and the system allow; they may require a network connection and are subject to their services’ privacy rules. System diagnostics and backups are not controlled by this app.

5. Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not offered in Apple’s Kids category. The store age rating is determined by the App Store Connect questionnaire and applicable regional ratings. The app does not verify users’ ages. We do not actively collect children’s personal information. Minors should use the app with a guardian’s guidance. If a guardian believes a child’s information was inadvertently sent to us through voluntary contact, please contact us at the email below.

6. Permissions

Core gameplay does not request location, camera, microphone, photo, contact or cross-app tracking permissions. Copying our email writes that address to the clipboard; it does not read existing clipboard contents. Contact opens your email client for you to review and send the message. Saves and device identifiers are not attached automatically. Sandbox file access and audio playback do not require sensitive permissions.

7. Policy Updates

This policy took effect and was updated on October 6, 2026, and applies to the current version. If network, account, advertising, analytics or real-money purchase features are added later, we will update this policy and store privacy disclosures before enabling them, and obtain authorization where required. This policy does not describe those unimplemented features as currently available.
